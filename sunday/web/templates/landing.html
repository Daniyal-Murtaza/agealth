☏For the parent: a phone that rings

At the time they chose, in their language, in the same warm voice. Sleep, meals, medication, then whatever they want to talk about. It remembers yesterday: "did the plumber come?" They can ring it too, any time, on a number saved as "Sunday".

✉For the family: a quiet page

A short note after each call, alerts when it matters, and a place to leave what you'd like asked tomorrow. "Remind Baba about the clinic." "Ask if she wants us on Sunday." Siblings stop coordinating by group chat.

✓Safe by code, not by prompt

"I fell", "chest pain", "can't breathe" are caught before any AI runs. The call stays on the line, keeps them calm, and every family member is alerted before the next sentence is spoken. Nothing is ever invented: every note quotes their own words.

What a call sounds like

Short turns. One question at a time. Her name now and then, not every sentence.

Sunday follows a loose plan (how are you, sleep, medication, meals, anything the family left, then open talk) and never asks twice for something she already said. Highlighted words are what the note to the family was built from.

How it works

  1. You introduce SundayAdd your parent, their language and call time, and who in the family gets which alerts. Place the first call from your page while you're with them.
  2. The phone rings"Hello Amma, it's Sunday, the daily call from your son Daniyal. How are you today?" Five to twelve minutes, one question at a time.
  3. You get the noteMood, medication, anything mentioned, anything worth a look. Replies to what you asked. On WhatsApp or SMS, with a link to read the call.
  4. Patterns over weeksEvery Sunday evening: how the week went, what to follow up on, what to talk about on your own call. Plain language, never a diagnosis.

Three rules, enforced in code

The parent never touches technology. No app, no account, nothing to charge.
Safety does not depend on the AI. Keyword triggers run first; emergencies follow a script and alert the family at once.
Nothing is invented. Every observation, alert and memory must quote the parent, or it is dropped.

Trust, visible

Always says it is an assistant sent by the family. Never gives medical advice. Never asks for money, card numbers or codes, and tells the parent so, which also trains them to hang up on real scammers. Recording only with the parent's consent, announced on every recorded call. Everything exportable and deletable by the family.

Questions families ask

Will my mother know it's not a person?

Yes, always. The first words of every call are "it's Sunday, the daily call from your son". If she asks, it says it is an assistant. It never pretends otherwise.

What if she doesn't answer?

Sunday tries once more fifteen minutes later, then leaves her alone for the day. Two mornings in a row with no answer and you get a "worth a call" note; three and the whole family gets an urgent alert.

What happens if she says she has fallen?

Before the AI is even consulted, every family member gets an urgent message quoting her words. Sunday stays on the line, tells her help is being called, and gives the local emergency number. It does not hang up.

Does it give medical advice?

Never. It shows concern, asks how she is managing, and tells the family. The doctor is the right person to ask, and Sunday says so.

Who can see the notes?

Only the family members you add, each at the level they choose: everything, check-today and urgent, or urgent only. Sign-in is a one-time link to their own phone. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Which languages?

English, Urdu, Hindi and Punjabi today, including the emergency phrases in each. The family's notes are in English.
