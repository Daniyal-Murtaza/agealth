Plain terms for a plain product.

What Sunday is

Sunday is an automated assistant that telephones an older person on behalf of their family, has a short conversation, and sends the family a summary. It always says it is an assistant sent by the family. It is not a medical device, an emergency service, or a substitute for human care.

What Sunday is not

  • It does not give medical advice and must not be relied on for any medical decision.
  • It is not a monitoring or emergency-response service. When a parent says something alarming, Sunday alerts the family members who asked for alerts and suggests the local emergency number; it does not call emergency services itself.
  • Calls can fail: no answer, a dropped line, a model outage. Sunday records what happened and tries once more, and the family must not assume silence means all is well.

Your responsibilities

  • You confirm you are a family member and that the parent knows Sunday will call. The first call is placed by you, with you on hand to introduce it.
  • You only add family members who should see the notes, and you keep your phone secure, since sign-in links are sent to it.
  • You do not use Sunday to call anyone who has not agreed to it.

Service and payment

Sunday is free during the beta. When paid plans begin we will give at least 30 days' notice and you may export your data and leave at any time without charge. See Pricing.

Liability

We provide Sunday with reasonable care but without warranty. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss arising from a missed or failed call, a delayed alert, or reliance on a note. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be limited by law.
