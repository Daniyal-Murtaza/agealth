Sunday is honest with the parent every single time, and records only when they have said yes.

Disclosure

Every call opens with the same fixed words: "it's Sunday calling, the daily call from your son Daniyal" (with the real relation and name). If the parent asks, Sunday says it is an assistant. It never pretends to be a person or a family member.

The introductory call

Daily calls do not begin until a signed-in family member has placed the first call from their page, so they can be with the parent to introduce Sunday and answer questions. A number typed into a form is never rung on its own.

Recording

  • Recording is off by default.
  • A family member switches it on only after the parent has agreed. We record who switched it on and when.
  • Every recorded call begins with "This call is recorded for the family, as you agreed", in the parent's language, so the parent hears it each time and can object.
  • Recordings are available only to signed-in family members and are deleted with the account.
  • Switch it off at any time from Settings; later calls are not recorded.

Withdrawing

If the parent no longer wants the calls, tell us or stop them from Settings. We will not ring again.
